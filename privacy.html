Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

"Full Send Ranked Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Full Send Ranked. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube upload access: to upload videos to the owner's own channel.
  • YouTube read-only access: to read the upload and processing status of the owner's own videos.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only access: to read the owner's own channel statistics.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish videos and measure the performance of the owner's own channel. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
